### Findings

1. **[P1] `/api/v1` daily Rupiah cap (`dailyIssueCapIdr`) bypassed by `POST /bulk-imports/{id}/commit` and `POST /shipments/{slug}/pay-unpaid`, and leaked on non-issuing `/issue` outcomes**
   - **Location:** `apps/worker/src/api-v1/index.ts:154-175`, `apps/worker/src/api-v1/index.ts:236-248`, `apps/worker/src/do/gerai-vault/index.ts:665,764`
   - **Failure scenario:**
     1. An owner creates an integration key with `scopes: ["shipments:issue"]` and `dailyIssueCapIdr = 50_000` (`spec 08 §2`). Calling `POST /api/v1/bulk-imports` (up to 200 rows) followed by `POST /api/v1/bulk-imports/{id}/commit` materializes ready INVs and enqueues `gx-bulk` `{ kind: "dispatch" }`, dispatching all 200 orders against the outlet's real Mengantar seller balance with zero `reserveDailySpend` check. Similarly, `POST /api/v1/shipments/{slug}/pay-unpaid` spends Mengantar balance via `POST /order/pay-unpaid` on held shipments without reserving against `dailyIssueCapIdr`.
     2. On `POST /api/v1/shipments/{slug}/issue` (`api-v1/index.ts:155-162`), `already` only checks `["issued", "provider_unpaid_hold"]`. Calling `/issue` on a `cancelled` shipment (`do/gerai-vault/index.ts:665`) or an ambiguous call that `reconcileDispatch` resets to `draft` (`do/gerai-vault/index.ts:764`) returns `r.ok === true`, skipping `releaseDailySpend` and permanently consuming the key's daily cap without an issued order. Thrown errors from `platformOf`/`dispatchShipment` also skip release, and separate `Date.now()` calls across 00:00 WIB midnight reserve and release on different `wib_day` rows.
   - **Smallest fix:** Capture `const now = Date.now()` once; include `"cancelled"` in `already`, wrap dispatch in `try/catch` to release on throw, and release whenever `r.ok && !["issued", "provider_unpaid_hold"].includes(r.value.state)`. Enforce `reserveDailySpend` (with release on failure) before `bulkMaterialize` in `POST /bulk-imports/{id}/commit` (summing ready rows' `counter_due_idr + effective_gerai_cost_idr`) and before `payUnpaid` in `POST /shipments/{slug}/pay-unpaid` (for `effective_gerai_cost_idr` when `dispatch_state === "provider_unpaid_hold"`).
